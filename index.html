The Non-Melting Story

Born from one sticky summer afternoon and a big idea.

Why we exist

It all started when our founder watched kids (and adults) struggle with melting ice pops on a scorching day. Sticky hands, ruined clothes, and sad little puddles everywhere.

We asked: “What if an ice pop never melted?”

After years of playful experiments, we cracked the code — a special freeze-lock formula that keeps every pop perfectly solid while still tasting incredibly fresh and fruity.

Founded by Misha Raha K

Visionary, flavor explorer, and lifelong enemy of sticky fingers.
